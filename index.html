Privacy notice

You do not need to enter your South African ID number or PSIRA number. This pilot stores audit and case records only on this device when you choose to save or create a report. Do not enter payslip images, identity numbers, bank details or passwords.

This prototype does not send reports to government departments, employers, unions, bargaining councils or inspectors. A future secure version will require a protected database and authorised access system.
